# Tech finance profile

Use this profile for items at the intersection of technology and money: the business of tech companies, venture capital, semiconductors, AI infrastructure economics, and markets driven by technology.

Typical items include: tech company earnings and guidance, funding rounds and venture capital dynamics, semiconductor industry news (chips, fab capacity, export controls, GPU supply), AI capex and infrastructure spending, tech M&A and IPOs, antitrust and regulation of tech platforms, startup strategy and business-model analysis, and market-moving tech events.

Also include analytical takes on these topics: strategy essays about platform economics, venture market analysis, and financial-structure explainers related to technology companies.

Do not use this profile for pure engineering content (model architectures, library releases, benchmarks without business context), routine price movements without a tech story, generic macro news with no technology angle, or promotional content.
